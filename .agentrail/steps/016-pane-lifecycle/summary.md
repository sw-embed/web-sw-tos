Implemented the pane lifecycle: ended applications keep their pane flagged ' (ended)' instead of being dropped, reusing a channel clears it first, ChannelOpen opens a titled pane (it was previously unhandled and set an error), and Ctrl-A c clears the focused pane. Nearly all of it lives in src/transport.rs rather than the vendored files, applying the previous step's re-vendoring lesson; 'ended' is carried in the pane title so there is no side table to keep in sync. One additive Desktop::clear_focused was unavoidable since emptying a pane needs a private field. 5 new tests.
