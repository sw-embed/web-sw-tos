SWTOS boots and runs in the browser: the real SPAWN banner and shell menu, produced by the emulated COR24 executing the vendored image. Added crates/swtos-host/src/pump.rs (the browser counterpart to the CLI adapter's loop) and src/session.rs. Measured throughput rather than assuming it: native 2.46 ms/tick, WASM 16.78 ms/tick with opt-level=z, against a 10 ms budget -- about 60 Hz instead of 100. Kept opt-level=z because it is both faster and smaller than opt-level=3 (176 KB vs 220 KB), the hot loop being an instruction decoder. Recorded what 60 Hz costs: shell latency and preemption unaffected, Uptime/Clock run 40% slow. Two measurement traps documented: a hidden tab throttles timers to ~1 Hz (first measurement reported a bogus 961 ms/tick), and work per callback must therefore be derived from the wall clock, hence catch-up capped at 20 ticks.
